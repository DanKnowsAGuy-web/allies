Utility incentives, Illinois

Commonwealth Edison Company (ComEd)IL

As of October 9, 2026

What this utility pays today

Rooftop unit replacement gets an instant discount at the distributor through ComEd Commercial HVAC Instant Discounts (midstream, through participating distributors), 2026: $75 per ton for a high efficiency rooftop unit from 5.4 to under 11.25 tons1. Large orders need an email notice to ComEd before the distributor invoice date1.

The compressor-cycling controller and the refrigerant-side treatment of existing cooling equipment have no confirmed fit yet; if ComEd Custom Incentives (Custom, including CHP; New Construction IT; Specialty/New Construction Lighting), program year 2026 accepts them, the rate would be $0.18 per kWh on verified first-year savings, with the pre-application done first2. ComEd's custom path turns on certified test results that ComEd accepts, so the product's test data would decide the answer3.

The all-in-one solar thermal system with storage has no confirmed fit, because ComEd Custom lists renewables among projects that are not eligible and the unit's rating for the instant discount is not on file4.

The demand charge

On Rate RDS for the large load delivery class, the 2026 charge is $12.78 & ADJ per kW of Maximum Kilowatts Delivered5. That is the highest 30-minute weekday demand between 9 in the morning and 6 in the evening5. A demand charge bills the highest kW the site pulls in a billing period, and a 10-ton rooftop unit's compressor starts add to that peak5. We found no ratchet in the 2026 ratebook, so each month's charge starts fresh5.

Demand response

Rider VLR, ComEd's voluntary load response tariff, pays for each kWh of load the site cuts during an event, at an amount ComEd sets for that event6. The tariff only commits to an amount greater than $0.00, while ComEd's flyer says at least $0.25 per kWh6. The site has to commit to at least a 10 kW load reduction, a group can enroll through a common representative, and ComEd pays once a year, by December 31; the money is for load reduction, never for equipment6.

Financing

Illinois C-PACE runs under the Property Assessed Clean Energy Act (50 ILCS 50), and any county or municipality may adopt the statewide Illinois Finance Authority PACE Program, run by its C-PACE Open Market Initiative7. Our count shows 13 of 24 service counties confirmed, including Cook, DuPage, Lake and McHenry7. The act names high efficiency heating, ventilating and air conditioning systems and energy controls, and C-PACE finances the project rather than paying a rebate7.

Watch

ComEd Custom incentives run until December 31, 2026 or until approved funds run out, first come, first served2. Standard projects must be complete with the final application in by December 31, 20268. Wait for the custom reservation letter before buying equipment, and sign and return the reservation form within 30 days2.

Next step

Ask ComEd for a written ruling on which certified test results it would accept for the custom path, since that answer decides whether the controller and the treatment get in9.

If we can't prove the savings, nothing is installed and you owe nothing.
