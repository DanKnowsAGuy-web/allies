What this utility pays today

The compressor-cycling controller, the refrigerant-side treatment of existing cooling equipment and the solar thermal system with storage would each go through the Smart $aver Custom Incentive Program (Florida), where the incentive is set project by project1. A written yes from the utility comes first, and the standards cap the incentive at 50% of the customer's total project cost1. Duke pays only on new equipment, which may rule out a treatment of existing equipment, so the refrigerant-side treatment is the least certain of the three2.

Rooftop unit replacement has a printed rate: Smart $aver Business Prescriptive Incentives (Florida) pays $50 per ton for unitary AC and heat pumps over 65,000 Btu/h that meet the required efficiencies3. No pre-approval step is stated for the HVAC measures3.

The demand charge

Duke's GSD-1 General Service Demand rate bills each kW of billing demand, which is the highest half-hour demand in the current billing period4. We read the current per-kW figure from the customer's bill. A demand charge bills the highest kW the site pulls in a billing period, and a rooftop unit's compressor starts add to that peak. The sheet does not carry a ratchet, so on a 10-ton rooftop unit each month's peak stands on its own4.

Demand response

Duke's CS-2 Curtailable General Service rate pays a Curtailable Demand Credit of $8.00 per kW of committed on-peak demand capability, plus an event incentive of 25¢ on each kWh the site drops during a curtailment, measured against the half hour before it5. To qualify, billing demand has to be 500 kW or more and the customer agrees to curtail 25% or more of average monthly billing demand5. It pays for load reduction, and it is not an equipment rebate.

Financing

Florida C-PACE runs through the Florida Development Finance Corporation with the Florida Resiliency and Energy District, the Florida PACE Funding Agency, and county or city administrators set up by local ordinance6. It names heating, cooling and ventilation systems that cut energy use, and building controls, among the improvements it finances, if the county takes part6. Our current count is at least 24 of 32 service counties, and we are still confirming it6. We don't have the statute text on file, so treat the legal details as not yet confirmed.
