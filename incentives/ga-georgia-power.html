Georgia PowerGA

As of October 9, 2026

What this utility pays today

The compressor-cycling controller and the all-in-one solar thermal system with storage can go through the Georgia Power custom upgrades program, which pays $0.11 per kWh on measured yearly savings up to 2,000,000 kWh and $0.07 per kWh above that once the utility approves the project1. Preapproval isn't required, but Georgia Power highly recommends it, so we get the utility's written yes before the customer buys anything1. The solar unit is less certain, since Georgia Power may treat solar heat as swapping another energy source for electricity2.

The refrigerant-side treatment of existing cooling equipment would go through the Georgia Power building calibration program, which pays on test-in and test-out savings at $0.11 per kWh up to 2,000,000 kWh and $0.07 per kWh above once the utility approves the project3. We are still confirming that fit with Georgia Power, so we get its written yes here too4.

Rooftop unit replacement pays $80 per ton through Georgia Power prescriptive heating and cooling rebates, for an air-source heat pump package from 65,000 to under 135,000 Btu/h5. Rebates on that list share a limit of $100,000 per building per year, up to 50% of equipment cost5.

The demand charge

We don't have Georgia Power's demand charge on file yet, so we read it from the customer's bill. A demand charge bills the highest kW the site pulls in a billing period, and a 10-ton rooftop unit's compressor starts add to that peak. That is the peak bucket of overspend; the price and the equipment are the other two.

Demand response

The Georgia Power Curtailable Load Rider is for customers who can drop at least 200 kW of demand when the company asks6. It pays monthly credits set in each customer's agreement, and Georgia Power hasn't published a per-kW rate6. It pays for load reduction, and it is not an equipment rebate.

Financing

Georgia C-PACE works county by county: a county or city can offer it only if it adopts its own program through Peach State C-PACE, the statewide administrator, and Invest Atlanta runs the City of Atlanta program7. It finances HVAC systems and building automation systems, and it is financing, not a rebate7. C-PACE is confirmed in at least 4 of 155 service counties, DeKalb, Douglas, Fulton and Putnam, and we confirm it for the address7. We don't have the statute text on file yet.
