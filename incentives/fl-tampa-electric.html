Tampa ElectricFL

As of October 9, 2026

What this utility pays today

Tampa Electric retired its commercial HVAC rebates on June 1, 2025, so every measure we sell now routes to the utility's custom rebate12. The compressor-cycling controller, the refrigerant-side treatment, rooftop unit replacement and the all-in-one solar thermal system with storage can all be considered there, because the custom rebate takes most commercially available demand reduction technologies that no other Tampa Electric offer covers2. There is no per-kW or per-kWh rate: Tampa Electric sets each rebate at a two-year simple payback or a RIM score of 1.01, whichever is more restrictive, and the project needs at least one kW of summer or winter demand reduction2. A written yes from the utility comes first, because no equipment can be bought until Tampa Electric issues the pre-qualification certificate2.

The demand charge

On GSD General Service - Demand, the standard option bills $19.06 per kW of billing demand in every season, set by the highest half-hour of demand in the billing period3. A site on primary voltage gets a discount of $1.42 per kW of billing demand, and the optional energy-only version prints $0.00 per kW of billing demand3. The tariff does not print a ratchet, so each month's charge rests on that month's peak3. A demand charge bills the highest kW the site pulls in a billing period, and on a 10-ton rooftop unit the compressor starts add to that peak.

Demand response

Tampa Electric's Demand Response (Commercial Demand Response) pays for load reduction, not for equipment: the utility's vendor pays the customer each month based on the kW of load reduction available at the facility4. The rate is not published, and a site needs at least 25 kW of transferable load by itself, since load from more than one facility cannot be combined to reach it4.

Financing

C-PACE reads yes in 4 of 4 service counties, offered through the Florida Development Finance Corporation (FDFC) with the Florida Resiliency and Energy District (FRED), the Florida PACE Funding Agency (FPFA), and county or city administrators5. We do not have the statute itself on file, but the definitions name energy-saving heating, cooling and ventilation systems and energy controls5. It is a way to finance the project, not a rebate, so check which administrator serves the property's address first.

Watch

Commercial Chiller was retired June 1, 2025, and its rebates went only to projects submitted before that date1. The GSLM-2/3 load management rider takes new subscriptions only if the annual need assessment shows new non-firm load, and it projects zero new customers for 2026 and 20276. The current conservation plan runs 2025 to 20342.
