What this utility pays today

Rooftop unit replacement pays $18.00 per ton for small businesses and $16.00 per ton for medium and large businesses through NV Energy Business Energy Services Retrofit, for a unit at the qualifying EER or IEER1. Every project needs pre-notification, and incentives are limited to 50% of project cost, or 90% for small businesses1.

The limited-time Advanced HVAC Optimization Controls bonus is written for machine-learning systems that coordinate multiple units, so the compressor-cycling controller has no confirmed fit yet on that bonus or the custom path, and we are asking NV Energy21. Retrofit savings must come from installed equipment and hold for 5 years, so the refrigerant-side treatment of existing cooling equipment has no confirmed fit, and we are asking NV Energy3. The retrofit rules say renewable energy projects are not eligible, so the all-in-one solar thermal system with storage has no confirmed fit, and we are asking NV Energy4.

The demand charge

On NV Energy's LGS-2 Large General Service schedule, for sites billing from 300 kW to 999 kW at secondary voltage, demand costs $13.43 per kW in the summer on-peak period from June to September and $1.65 per kW in winter5. A demand charge bills the highest kW the site pulls in a billing period, and a 10-ton rooftop unit's compressor starts add to that peak. A facilities charge of $3.45 per kW also bills the highest demand of the current and prior twelve billing periods, so one high summer month stays on the bill for a year5. That is the peak bucket of overspend; the price and the equipment are the other two.

Demand response

The NV Energy Optional Load Management rider pays a fixed credit of $7.50 per summer month for a receiver switch or $5 per summer month for a communicating thermostat, plus an hourly payment during events6. It sets no minimum size, only a cap of 10 tons per central AC compressor, and it pays for load reduction, not for equipment6.

Financing

Nevada's C-PACE statute, added by 2021 SB 283 and in force, lets a city or county create a district to finance qualifying improvements7. C-PACE is confirmed in at least 1 of 2 service counties, Clark, for its unincorporated areas, and we confirm it for the address7. It covers permanently affixed improvements that cut energy use or demand, and it is financing, not a rebate7.
