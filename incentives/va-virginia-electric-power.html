Dominion Energy VirginiaVA

As of October 9, 2026

What this utility pays today

For a small business that qualifies, rooftop unit replacement pays $29 per ton through the Dominion Small Business Improvement Enhanced Program, or $56 per ton at the higher efficiency tier, so we check the site's demand history first1. It starts with an on-site assessment by a participating contractor, and the rebate is capped at 75% of the customer's total invoice amount1.

The compressor-cycling controller on a rooftop unit, the refrigerant-side treatment of existing cooling equipment and the all-in-one solar thermal system with storage can each go through Dominion Custom Energy Solutions, which pays $0.12 per kWh on the savings it accepts once the utility approves the project2. Every project is approved before work starts, and the payment is capped at 75% of the customer's total invoice amount2.

The demand charge

On Schedule GS-2 Intermediate General Service, the distribution demand charge is $4.963 per kW on all kW of demand, all year3. Generation demand is its own line at $1.803 per kW from June to September and $0.588 per kW from October to May, and transmission demand is its own line at $1.971 per kW3. A demand charge bills the highest kW the site pulls in a billing period, and a 10-ton rooftop unit's compressor starts add to that peak. Once a site has reached 500 kW, its minimum demand holds at the highest demand from the current and previous eleven billing months3. That is the peak bucket of overspend; the price and the equipment are the other two.

Demand response

The Dominion Non-Residential Curtailment Program pays for load reduction, not for equipment4. Dominion's program page lists $36 per kW per year, paid monthly, for sites with at least 100 kW of curtailable load at peak, but the program's terms don't set a rate, so we confirm it before quoting a site4. In a month with no event, the payment is the monthly rate times enrolled capacity, and in an event month it is the rate times the average curtailment across event hours4. Enrollment has been open since January 1, 2026, and a site stays enrolled until it withdraws4.

Financing

C-PACE is enacted and in force in Virginia, and the Virginia PACE Authority, a nonprofit, administers the statewide program with the Virginia Department of Energy as sponsor5. Each locality opts in by ordinance5. C-PACE is confirmed in at least 22 of 103 service counties and we confirm it for the address5. Common eligible improvements include HVAC upgrades, new building and HVAC controls, new packaged units and solar thermal5.
