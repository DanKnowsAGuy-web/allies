Utility incentives, Alabama

Alabama PowerAL

As of October 9, 2026

What this utility pays today

Alabama Power has no HVAC rebate for a business in 2026 and its only commercial rebate covers electric cooking equipment1. That means the compressor-cycling controller, the refrigerant-side treatment, rooftop unit replacement and the all-in-one solar thermal system with storage each have no path to a utility rebate here1. The money in Alabama sits in the bill itself, so lead with the demand charge and the interruptible rider.

The demand charge

On Rate BTA Business Time Advantage (Optional), Alabama Power bills $4.74 per kW of billing capacity in every month2. Billing capacity is the highest fifteen-minute demand in the billing period, and a ratchet keeps it from dropping below a share of the peak set in June through September2. The charge is reduced by fifty-four cents (54¢) per kW of billing capacity when the customer supplies the transformers off distribution lines, or reduced by one dollar and thirty cents ($1.30) per kW of billing capacity off transmission lines2. A demand charge bills the highest kW the site pulls in a billing period, and on a 10-ton rooftop unit the compressor starts add to that peak. That makes the peak the first of the three buckets to look at here, ahead of the price and the equipment.

Demand response

Rate Rider IC, the interruptible rider, pays $2.55, $2.02 or $0.98 per kW, and that money is for load reduction, not for equipment1. Alabama Power's Commercial and Industrial Automated Demand Response is still under development, so there is no rate or kW threshold to quote yet3. Treat it as a future option and keep the conversation on Rate Rider IC.

Financing

Alabama's C-PACE law is enacted, but no county has a verified program or administrator, so it is not available anywhere we can confirm: 0 of 59 service counties4. The law does name energy-saving heating, cooling and ventilation systems and energy controls as qualifying improvements, so it could finance a project if a county opts in, and it would be a way to pay for the project, not a rebate4.

Watch

No dated closing or deadline is on file for Alabama Power.

Next step

No written ruling is on file for this territory, so start with the customer's bill: pull the demand line and the rate schedule, ask whether the site could sign up for Rate Rider IC, and size the peak from there.
