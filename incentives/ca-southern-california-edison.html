What this utility pays today

The compressor-cycling controller on a rooftop unit, the refrigerant-side treatment of existing cooling equipment and rooftop unit replacement could each go through the SCE Measured Savings Program, which pays an aggregator for metered savings1. No per-measure rate is published, and SCE pays the aggregator rather than the customer, so we are asking how the customer's share is set1. SCE caps the payment at 120% of the incentive it reserves when it approves the project, and may add a cap tied to project cost1.

The all-in-one solar thermal system with storage has no confirmed fit, because nobody has settled whether the collector and the storage count toward savings, so we are asking SCE2. Midstream money for solar thermal collectors is not available this year, since those funds are exhausted3.

The demand charge

On TOU-GS-2, for sites above 20 kW and below 200 kW, the Option D facilities demand charge is 4.90 dollars per kW for transmission and 23.11 dollars per kW for distribution each month4. Summer on-peak demand, weekdays from 4 to 9 p.m., adds 12.54 dollars per kW for delivery and 12.86 dollars per kW for generation4. A demand charge bills the highest kW the site pulls in a billing period, and a 10-ton rooftop unit's compressor starts add to that peak. There is no ratchet, so each month bills on that month's own maximum demand4. That is the peak bucket of overspend; the price and the equipment are the other two.

Demand response

The SCE agricultural and pumping interruptible rate pays a monthly bill credit per kW per meter for load SCE can interrupt, which pays for load reduction and is not an equipment rebate5. The credit is 18.46 dollars per kW for the summer average on-peak and 9.14 dollars per kW for the winter average mid-peak, for accounts with measured demand of 37 kW or greater5. It is written for agricultural and pumping accounts, so we check the account type first.

Financing

C-PACE is enacted and in force in California under the Streets and Highways Code, and each city, county or other public agency opts in6. Our current count is still being confirmed, so treat C-PACE as decided county by county and city by city, and we confirm it for the address6. It finances renewable generation and upgrades that cut overspend on power or water when they are permanently fixed to the property, and appliances that are not fixed do not qualify6.
