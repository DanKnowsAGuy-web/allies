What this utility pays today

At an APS site the savings come from the bill itself, because APS stopped paying HVAC incentives to business customers on January 1, 2026, after the Arizona Corporation Commission ordered them discontinued in Decision 815841. Only commitments signed before that date still get paid1.

The compressor-cycling controller and the refrigerant-side treatment of existing cooling equipment have no confirmed fit in APS Solutions for Business custom rebates yet, so we quote nothing and we are asking APS2.

Rooftop unit replacement has no confirmed path for a general business customer, because the 2026 general measures carry no unitary AC or heat pump line1. APS still lists a unitary line for schools under APS Solutions for Schools, but that line conflicts with the order, so we are asking APS whether it survives3.

The solar thermal system with storage has no confirmed path either, since no line pays for the solar assist and APS does not rebate renewables, so we are asking APS1.

The demand charge

We don't have APS's demand charge on file yet, so we read it from the customer's bill. A demand charge bills the highest kW the site pulls in a billing period, and a 10-ton rooftop unit's compressor starts add to that peak. That is the peak bucket of overspend; the price and the equipment are the other two.

Demand response

Cool Rewards Business pays a $60 one-time enrollment bill credit per smart thermostat and $40 each year per thermostat, up to $100 in bill credits the first year4. APS hasn't published a minimum kW, since eligibility runs per thermostat on central air conditioning4. It pays for enrolling cooling load in demand response, and it is not an equipment rebate.

Financing

Arizona does not have a C-PACE statute in force: HB2824 was held in Rules in 2026, so there is no administrator and no list of qualifying improvements5. C-PACE is confirmed in 0 of 14 service counties and we confirm it for the address if the law changes5.

Watch

No dated closing or deadline is on file for APS.

Next step

No written ruling is on file for this territory, so the next step is a recent APS bill, and we'll show the customer what the price, the peak and the equipment are costing them.

If we can't prove the savings, nothing is installed and you owe nothing.
