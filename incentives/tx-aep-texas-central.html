What this utility pays today

Rooftop unit replacement is on the deemed list of the AEP Texas Small Business Direct Install Program, and with no published rate for that line, CLEAResult quotes the amount once the job qualifies12. Every project needs pre-authorization, and the incentive is limited to 80% of project cost2.

AEP Texas also runs the Commercial Standard Offer Program, which pays on the verified peak kW a project takes off the bill, plus the kWh it saves3.

The Small Business list does not name the compressor-cycling controller, so it has no confirmed fit, and we are asking AEP Texas4. AEP Texas CoolSaver, the commercial A/C calibration offer, neither lists nor bars a refrigerant-side treatment of existing cooling equipment, so the treatment has no confirmed fit, and we are asking AEP Texas5. The packaged unit in the all-in-one solar thermal system with storage could use the unitary air conditioner line, but nothing credits the solar assist or storage, so the system has no confirmed fit, and we are asking AEP Texas6.

The demand charge

On AEP Texas's Secondary Voltage Service Greater Than 10 kW schedule, the distribution charge is $7.148 per kW of billing demand7. A demand charge bills the highest kW the site pulls in a billing period, and a 10-ton rooftop unit's compressor starts add to that peak. The distribution charge has no ratchet; it bills the month's highest 15-minute reading7. That is the peak bucket of overspend; the price and the equipment are the other two.

Demand response

The lever here is a charge the site avoids rather than a payment: once a site has peaked at 700 kW or more in any earlier month, Rider TCRF bills it $6.232736 per kW of its average demand at the ERCOT summer peaks8. Cutting load at those peaks lowers it, and it is not an equipment rebate8.

Financing

Texas C-PACE is run by the nonprofit Texas PACE Authority with the city or county that opts in under Local Government Code chapter 3999. C-PACE is confirmed in at least 1 of 44 service counties, Hidalgo, and we confirm it for the address9. We don't have the statute text on file yet. It is financing for permanent improvements that cut energy use or demand, not a rebate9.
