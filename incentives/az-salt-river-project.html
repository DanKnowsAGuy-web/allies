What this utility pays today

Rooftop unit replacement pays $60 per ton for Tier 1 or $85 per ton for Tier 2 through SRP Standard Business Solutions, at the highest tier the new unit's AHRI rating meets1. SRP doesn't require pre-qualification for HVAC equipment, and a rebate reservation holds funds for 90 days1.

The compressor-cycling controller and the refrigerant-side treatment of existing cooling equipment both fit SRP Custom Business Solutions, which pays $0.11 per kWh of first-year savings once the utility approves the project2. Retrofits need SRP's written approval before anything is ordered, and the rebate cannot go past 75% of incremental project cost2.

The solar thermal system with storage has no confirmed fit, because SRP's custom rules exclude savings that come from swapping another energy source for electricity, so we are asking SRP how it reads that rule for this unit3.

The demand charge

SRP's E-32 Standard Price Plan for Time-of-Use General Service bills demand on every kW over 5 kW4. The on-peak demand charge totals $5.05 for each kW in the May, June, September and October billing cycles, $6.75 in July and August, and $4.45 from November through April4. A separate charge of $1.02 for each kW bills on the higher of the shoulder-peak and off-peak readings in every season4. A demand charge bills the highest kW the site pulls in a billing period, and a 10-ton rooftop unit's compressor starts add to that peak. That is the peak bucket of overspend; the price and the equipment are the other two.

Demand response

SRP hasn't published a rate, a minimum kW or a payment basis for the SRP Business Demand Response Program, so we are asking SRP for each site's terms5. It pays for load reduction, and it is not an equipment rebate.

Financing

Arizona does not have a C-PACE statute in force: HB2824 was held in Rules in 2026, so there is no administrator and no list of qualifying improvements6. C-PACE is confirmed in 0 of 3 service counties and we confirm it for the address if the law changes6.

Watch

  • April 30, 2027: the program year for SRP Custom Business Solutions ends2.
  • October 31, 2027: SRP's application workbook gives this as the late-application deadline, which differs from its manual, so we confirm which one applies1.

Next step

No written ruling is on file for this territory, so the next step is a recent SRP bill, and we'll show the customer what the price, the peak and the equipment are costing them.

If we can't prove the savings, nothing is installed and you owe nothing.
